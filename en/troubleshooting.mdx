---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Check sharing, permissions, recognition, sync, and restore when something does not work as expected."
---

## Galpi is missing from the share sheet

1. Open **Share** in Safari, YouTube, or another app.
2. Open **More** at the end of the app list.
3. Use **Edit** to enable Galpi and add it to favorites.
4. If it is still missing, restart the device and check again.

## Voice or handwriting capture is unavailable

Check microphone, Speech Recognition, Camera, and Photos permissions in the device's **Settings > Apps > Galpi**. Return to Galpi and try capture again after changing a permission.

## Handwriting recognition is inaccurate

- Compare the result with the original image in the thought's detail screen.
- Exclude background or unrelated writing in the selection editor.
- Try changing the basic recognition language-correction option.
- If needed, choose advanced recognition after reviewing its external-transfer notice.

## The audio is saved but there is no transcript

- Play the original from the thought's detail screen to check the saved recording.
- Check Speech Recognition permission and the recognition language.
- First use of a language may need an internet connection and storage for its model.
- Wait if processing is still in progress, or use the retry action when shown.
- If the device does not support transcription, you can listen to the original and type the body yourself.

## YouTube caption import or translation is not completing

- Check the internet connection.
- Caption import does not depend on a personal Gemini API key. A video without public captions cannot be imported.
- Restricted videos or an unavailable relay service can prevent caption import.
- For Korean or English translation, check the required language model and available storage.
- Follow any error-specific retry guidance in the app.

## iCloud sync appears stuck

1. Check the device's Apple account and iCloud Drive status.
2. Read the status and pending counts in **Settings > iCloud sync**.
3. Request a sync now.
4. If the issue continues, make a ZIP backup and note the app version and exact status message.

## Restored data has not appeared

After restore preparation completes, fully quit Galpi and reopen it; switching to another app is not enough. If validation failed, choose another backup. A failed validation does not change current data.

## Routine notifications do not arrive

- Check enabled reminders and weekdays in **Settings > Routine**.
- Allow notifications in the device's **Settings > Notifications > Galpi**.
- Leave the routine settings screen after changing the time so updated scheduling can be applied.
- Open Galpi directly to record even when reminders are off.

## Galpi is missing from widgets or controls

Open Galpi once, then return to the widget or Control Center picker. If it is still missing, restart the device and check that the latest Galpi build is installed.

## An imported conversation has no body

The shared page may be inaccessible or its format may have changed. Copy the conversation and use the paste-original action in its Galpi detail screen.

## Keep useful problem details

Note your device model, OS version, Galpi version from **Settings > App information**, exact error message, and the steps that caused it. Check screenshots before sharing so they do not expose private records or API keys.

If records appear missing, check Trash and sync status first and [back up](/en/sync-and-backup) the current data. Deleting the app before checking your backup can remove originals stored only on that device.
