---
title: "Start small"
description: "A thought does not have to be polished. Leave only what you want to leave today."
---

Open **Take a moment** from Home. Choose **Say what’s on your mind** to speak freely, or try the **Start small** paper activity. These are optional starting points, not a program you have to complete.

## Talk it out

Open voice capture and start with whatever is still on your mind. It is fine to repeat yourself or change direction.

- What stayed with you from something you saw or heard today?
- What sounded good but did not quite fit your own view?
- What would you like to explain to someone right now?

When you have no more to say, save and stop. You do not need to turn the transcript into polished writing. See [Capture a thought](/en/capture-thoughts) for the controls.

## Put something on paper

The **Start small** card shows a short prompt. Use the next-prompt action to choose another. If a new notebook feels intimidating, any scrap of paper will do.

- Write the names of three objects you can see.
- Leave a sound you just heard as words or lines.
- Put three words that come to mind anywhere on the page.

Use the photo action only if you want to keep the page, or choose to finish for today. Taking or uploading a photo is optional. A full page or neat handwriting is not required.

## Return when you want

A few days later, open one record in Thoughts and notice whether it still feels the same. The original audio or page can help you remember how you expressed it.

If something new comes to mind, choose **Add a thought** on the card or record detail. The new voice, handwriting, or text record is linked to the earlier record while keeping its original intact. Reading and closing the app is fine too.

You do not have to publish what you make. If there is something you want to share, try [handwriting image sharing](/en/handwriting-library).
