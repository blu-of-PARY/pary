## Review a source when you want

Inbox holds your saved sources. You do not need to empty it or read everything. Open what interests you now and choose whether to:

- Leave a connected thought.
- View it later.
- Archive it.
- Move it to Trash.

Use the undo action when offered after moving an item. Trash items can be restored within 30 days.

## Connect topics

Open **Topics** from the Home menu to create groups for interests or projects. Connect thoughts and sources to a topic from their detail screens.

A topic with linked records cannot be deleted immediately. Remove its record memberships first.

## Search

1. Open **Search**.
2. Choose thoughts, sources, or both.
3. Narrow the record types with the search-scope controls.
4. Enter a word or phrase.

Search checks saved text such as thought bodies, edited recognition results, source titles and bodies, and stored YouTube transcripts. Audio and images whose transcription or recognition has not finished may not be searchable by their contents yet.

Try words you remember from the record. Galpi does not provide an AI conversation that finds records by meaning.

<Tip>
If there are too many results, narrow the thought/source scope first, then keep only the source types you need.
</Tip>

## Change the Thoughts view

- **List** lets you skim text and connections.
- **Gallery** emphasizes images and handwriting.
- **Calendar** shows records by date.

Filter by handwriting, voice, or text and by today, the last seven days, or the last 30 days. Sort by newest, oldest, or recently edited. You can select multiple thoughts to move to Trash together.

See [Archive and Trash](/en/manage-library) for keeping and restoring records.
