title: "Choose your rhythm"
description: "Use optional reminders on the days and at the times that suit you."
---

Routines help you open capture at a chosen time. You do not need a daily schedule or to make up missed days. You can record whenever you want without notifications.

## Set a routine

1. Open **Settings** from Home.
2. Open **Routine**.
3. Choose a rhythm close to your everyday life.

| Rhythm | Starting point |
| --- | --- |
| **Evening reflection** | Leave a thought before bed and return in the morning. |
| **Morning focus** | Start around the same morning time. |
| **A little every day** | Set aside a short evening moment. |
| **Weekend morning** | Leave more room on a weekend morning. |

You can change the time, days, and input method after choosing a preset. Custom changes appear as a custom routine.

## Time to leave a thought

Enable reminders and choose the time and weekdays. Start with every day or weekends and adjust individual days. Turning off the last selected day also turns off this reminder.

Allow notifications when the system asks. Declining permission does not stop you from recording in Galpi.

## Return in the morning

The morning reminder invites you to leave a line about what comes to mind after sleep. You can also start it from Home later. It does not automatically summarize yesterday's records.

## Default input method

Choose the method opened by routine reminders and the routine entry point on Home:

- **Voice** to think aloud.
- **Typing** to write directly.

Start handwriting from the general capture menu or use direct writing on iPad.

<Tip>
Choose a moment that already feels natural, such as after reading or before bed. If notifications feel like pressure, turn them off and use [quick capture](/en/settings-and-shortcuts) instead.
</Tip>
