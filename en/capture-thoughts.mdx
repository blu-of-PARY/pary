## Choose a recognition language

Open **Settings > Recognition language** and choose Korean, English, or Device language. This setting applies to both voice transcription and handwriting recognition, separately from the interface language.

The default, **Device language**, uses the first Korean or English entry in your device's preferred-language list. If neither is listed, it uses English. An explicit selection takes priority over device preferences.

A change applies to newly started recognition and tasks you manually retry. It does not automatically change existing originals or recognized text.

## Type a thought

1. Choose text capture from **Home**.
2. Write what is on your mind.
3. Save the record.

You do not need a title or a topic first. You can edit the text and connect it to sources or topics later.

## Speak a thought

1. Choose voice capture from **Home**.
2. Allow microphone access and tap **Start recording**.
3. Say what you want to leave, then save. Saving also stops the recording.

Galpi saves the original audio first and then transcribes it. Even while transcription is pending, you can play the saved audio from the thought's detail screen. Editing the transcript does not change the audio.

The first transcription may need Speech Recognition permission and a language model download. Check processing status or retry instructions in the thought's detail screen. A failed transcription does not mean you need to record the saved audio again.

<Note>
If you need to leave, pause and choose **Pause and leave**. You can resume from Home. Canceling capture discards an unsaved recording, so save or explicitly pause and leave when you want to keep it.
</Note>

## Capture handwriting

- **Write on screen**: use Apple Pencil or your finger on iPad.
- **Take photos**: capture several paper pages in sequence.
- **Choose photos**: import handwriting images from Photos.

After capture, you can include the handwriting you want and exclude unwanted areas. Multiple pages are stored in order within one record.

### Choose recognition

- **Basic recognition** uses Apple Vision on the device without a usage limit.
- **Advanced recognition** uses Gemini. Images are sent to Google, so Galpi asks for consent before enabling it.

Choose a default in **Settings > Handwriting recognition**. It applies to newly saved handwriting. Basic recognition does not require an internet connection. Advanced recognition needs a connection and available app-provided usage or a personal API key. See [Recognition and AI](/en/ai-features).

<Warning>
Recognition can differ from the original. Check quotations, names, and numbers against your page.
</Warning>

## Read or edit later

Open a record in **Thoughts** to see its original and editable body. For voice and handwriting, the detail menu also opens the original recognition result before your corrections.

You do not have to correct every word. You can fix just the names or words that matter when you need to find the thought again.

## Add what you think now

Choose **Add a thought** in a record's detail screen, then select voice, handwriting, or text. Saving creates a new record linked to the earlier one. It does not overwrite the earlier body or original, so you can revisit each thought separately.
