## Save from another app

When you find something in Safari, YouTube, Instagram, or another app:

1. Tap **Share** in that app.
2. Choose **Galpi**.
3. Add a note or topic if you want.
4. Save and return to the original app.

Galpi keeps the source URL and, when available, adds a title, author or channel, and thumbnail. A missing preview does not prevent saving the shared URL or text. Saving a link does not download the entire video or post. If the source is deleted or becomes private, you may not be able to open it later.

<Tip>
If Galpi is missing from the share sheet, open **More** at the end of the app list and add Galpi to your favorites.
</Tip>

## Add from Inbox

Open the add-source action in **Inbox** and choose a method.

### Photos and files

Select up to 20 images or screenshots and add a description, note, or topic.

### Links and text

Paste a URL or some text. Galpi can fetch preview information for URLs.

### Books and papers

Find a book by title, author, or ISBN, or a paper by title, author, or DOI. Select a result and optionally attach a cover or barcode photo.

## Open a source

A source's detail screen brings together the original, preview, note, and linked thoughts. Start a thought there to create a text, voice, or handwriting record connected to that source.

For YouTube sources, import publicly available captions without a personal Gemini API key. Caption timestamps open the corresponding position in the video. The language menu also lets you read the original or request a Korean or English translation. See [Recognition and AI](/en/ai-features).

You can edit a source's title, URL, and body, manage a short note, and connect topics. Editing the URL preserves thought links.

## Choose a reminder

When saving from another app, you can choose a revisit reminder:

- **Tomorrow**: schedule a reminder for the following day.
- **Choose date and time**: pick a future time.
- **None**: save to Inbox without a reminder.

For readable web pages, turn on the option to save the page body as well as the URL.

See [Import existing records](/en/import-notes-and-conversations) for Apple Notes and AI conversations, or [Books and passages](/en/books-and-quotes) for repeated notes about one book.
