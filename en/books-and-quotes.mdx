## Add a book

Choose book search from the add-source action in **Inbox**. Search by title, author, or ISBN, select a result, and save it. For another record about the same book, open the saved book instead of adding another search result.

## Keep writing about the same book

1. Find the saved book in **Inbox** or **Search**.
2. Choose to leave a thought.
3. Use text, voice, or handwriting.
4. Next time you read the book, start another thought from that same source.

Records created this way are linked to the book. Expand the book's linked content to find and open them.

## Keep the passage and your response clear

When capturing a memorable page, make it clear which words are the author's and which are your response. You can add the book title and page number in the thought's organization and source settings.

Several photos captured together are stored in order in one record. If you want to browse passages separately, create a separate record for each passage from the same book.

<Note>
Galpi currently groups a book and its records through links. It does not automatically merge duplicate books or split photos into passages and page numbers for a reading notebook.
</Note>

Before quoting or sharing a page, check its book, author, and page information and choose what to include.
