## Adjust the selected handwriting

After taking or selecting photos, open the selection editor to choose the handwriting to keep.

- Use **Include** to paint over handwriting you want.
- Use **Exclude** to remove unwanted background, shadows, or words from the selection.
- Adjust the brush size for small strokes or wider areas.
- Undo, fit the page, or reset the selection when needed.

For multiple pages, use the page selector to review each one. Selection edits preserve the original photos.

## Browse your handwriting

Open the handwriting area or its view-all action from the Home garden to browse the handwriting gallery. Choose a dated card to open the related thought.

## Share a handwriting image

1. Open a handwritten thought and choose its share action.
2. Choose an image format.
3. Turn the Galpi logo on or off.
4. Check the preview and number of pages.
5. Share to Photos, Messages, or another app.

| Format | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto** | Fits the length of the handwriting. |
| **Story** | Creates a tall image for a story-sized screen. |
| **Feed** | Splits longer handwriting across more square-shaped images. |

## Change the handwriting font

Open **Settings > Handwriting font** to preview and select an included font. You can also import a TTF or OTF file.

The selected font also applies to handwritten interface areas such as titles, buttons, and hints. Original text, input values, dates, and counts use a regular reading font.

Imported fonts stay on this device. Import them separately on another device if needed. Check the font provider's license before using or sharing a font.
