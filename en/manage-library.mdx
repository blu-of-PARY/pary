---
title: "Archive and Trash"
description: "Keep sources out of your active lists and restore mistakenly deleted items within 30 days."
---

Open the archive from the Home menu, then switch between archived items and Trash at the top.

## Archive sources

Archive sources you want to keep without seeing them in the active Inbox. The archive separates:

- Sources.
- Apple Notes imports.
- AI conversations.

Swipe an archived row and restore it when you want it back. Connected thoughts and attachments remain linked while it is archived.

## Move items to Trash

Swipe left on a thought or source and choose the Trash action. In Thoughts, you can also select several records and move them together.

For a recently moved thought, the undo action at the bottom of the screen restores it immediately.

## Restore from Trash

1. Open the archive from the Home menu.
2. Switch to **Trash**.
3. Swipe an item and choose **Restore**.

Each item shows the time remaining before permanent deletion. Items older than 30 days are permanently removed when Galpi next runs its cleanup after opening.

<Warning>
Permanent deletion removes the record's original audio or images, searchable text, and related connections or attachments. It cannot be undone.
</Warning>
