## Appearance

In **Settings**, choose to follow the system appearance or use a light or dark screen.

## Recognition language

In **Settings > Recognition language**, choose Korean, English, or Device language. This controls voice transcription and handwriting recognition separately from the interface language. See [recognition language](/en/capture-thoughts).

## Add a Home Screen widget

1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen.
2. Open **Edit > Add Widget**.
3. Search for **Galpi**.
4. Choose a size and add it.

Tap the small widget to open voice capture. The medium widget offers **Open** for a recent source, **Speak** for voice capture, and **Write** for text capture.

The widget opens the app's capture screen. Tap **Start recording** there to begin recording.

## Add a Control Center button

1. Open Control Center.
2. Touch and hold an empty area or tap the add button.
3. Search for Galpi in the controls list.
4. Add a voice, text, or handwriting capture control.

Control Center and Lock Screen controls open the corresponding screen in Galpi. Opening voice capture does not start recording automatically.

## Use Shortcuts

In the Shortcuts app, find Galpi's capture actions and add them to a shortcut, automation, or Home Screen icon. Choose voice, text, or handwriting.

<Tip>
Keep your most-used capture action on the Lock Screen or in Control Center to reduce the number of choices before you begin.
</Tip>

## Data and service settings

- **Settings > Privacy** manages detailed-reflection consent and saved results.
- **Settings > Advanced settings** manages your personal Gemini API key.
- **Settings > App information** shows the app version and third-party font and software notices.

See [Recognition and AI](/en/ai-features) for optional external features.
