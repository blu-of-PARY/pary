---
title: "Import existing records"
description: "Bring Apple Notes and ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini conversations into Galpi while keeping the imported original."
---

Imported material is kept as its own record first. You can then choose what to keep as a thought or a reference source.

## Import Apple Notes

1. Open a note in Apple Notes.
2. Tap **Share** and choose **Galpi**.
3. Check that the destination is Apple Notes and save.
4. Open the imported note in Galpi to see the shared original and any included folder information.

From an imported note, you can:

- Leave a follow-up thought by text, voice, or handwriting.
- Keep it as an editable thought.
- Keep it as a reference source.
- Exclude it from search or include it again.
- Connect topics and attachments.

Creating a thought or source from an import does not overwrite the imported original.

## Import AI conversations

Galpi can receive publicly accessible shared conversation pages from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

1. Open the conversation's share page.
2. Share it to **Galpi**.
3. Enable the option to save the conversation body.
4. Open the saved conversation in Galpi.

If the page could not be read, copy the conversation and paste it manually. Select messages from the stored original and mark what you want to adopt:

- A thought of your own.
- Reference information.
- A decision.
- An open question.

You can keep selected messages as separate thoughts or sources while leaving the conversation original intact.

<Note>
Permissions and changes to another service's shared-page format can prevent automatic import. Use pasted text when needed. Importing a link does not give Galpi access to your private conversation history.
</Note>
