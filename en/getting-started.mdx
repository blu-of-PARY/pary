## What you need

- An iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.
- An Apple account signed in on the device with iCloud available if you want iCloud sync.
- An internet connection for advanced handwriting recognition, YouTube caption import, detailed monthly reflections, and online source search.
- Speech recognition or translation may need to download a language model before first use.

Home shows your thoughts and sources as a monthly garden. You can also resume a paused recording or start something you want to leave today.

## Permissions

Galpi asks for permissions when you use the related feature.

- **Microphone** records your voice.
- **Speech Recognition** turns saved audio into text on the device.
- **Camera and Photos** let you capture paper or select an image.
- **Location** helps match the garden's sun and moon to local sunrise and sunset. Galpi does not store the location itself.
- **Notifications** deliver the reminders you choose.

To change permissions later, open your device's **Settings > Apps > Galpi**.

## Make your first record

1. Choose text, voice, or handwriting from **Home**.
2. Type, record, or capture your page.
3. Save the record.
4. Open **Thoughts** to find it.

<Tip>
Add a Galpi shortcut or a Control Center or Lock Screen control to reach capture faster. These open Galpi's capture screen. Voice recording begins when you tap **Start recording** in the app.
</Tip>
