## Your monthly garden

Home brings the month's thoughts and collected sources into one scene.

- Thoughts and sources appear as flowers and leaves.
- Move between months to see earlier gardens.
- The sun, moon, and sky change with the time of day and sunrise and sunset.

Tap a leaf or flower for a preview, then open it to visit the thought or source.

<Note>
You do not need to fill empty days or complete the garden. Take a look when there is something you want to return to.
</Note>

## Resume a record

Home lets you return to a paused voice recording or a thought you want to leave today. Open a paused recording to continue speaking or save it.

## Revisit saved sources

Galpi can suggest up to two sources a day that were saved at least three days ago and do not yet have a linked thought. You do not have to finish the suggestions or read every source.

1. Open a source suggestion on Home or the revisit action in **Inbox**.
2. Read the preview. Open the original if you want more context.
3. Choose to leave a thought.
4. Save text, voice, or handwriting to connect it to that source.

If a one-sentence reflection appears after saving, you can write it or skip it. Choose to view the source later to make it eligible again in three days.

## Take a moment

Open **Take a moment** from Home to see up to three records made before today. Cards show a portion of your saved text, not a new AI interpretation or summary. If a record has no text yet, you can open its original.

- Open the original and record to read the transcript, view the image, or play the audio.
- Choose **Add a thought** to leave a new voice, handwriting, or text record. The earlier record stays unchanged and the two are connected.
- Choose **Say what’s on your mind** to start a new voice record without linking it to a past record.

If you have no earlier records, try a paper activity in **Start small**. Today's records can become eligible from the next day. Records in Trash are not suggested.

## Find your own thoughts

Open a recent record in **Thoughts**, or use **Search** for a word you remember. For voice records, skim the transcript first and play the original if useful. You do not have to listen to the whole recording.

## Monthly reflections

Open a reflection from a past month's garden to see the flow of your records. You can save or share a reflection card as an image.

Detailed AI reflections are created only when you request one and consent to external analysis. See [Recognition and AI](/en/ai-features).
