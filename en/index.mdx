Galpi is an iPhone and iPad app for thoughts you write by hand, speak aloud, or type. Keep your original pages and recordings, then read or search their text when you want to revisit a thought.

<span className="galpi-section-kicker">A SMALL BEGINNING</span>

## Find your starting point

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Get started" icon="sprout" href="/en/getting-started">
    Find your way around the four main screens and make your first record.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Capture a thought" icon="pen-line" href="/en/capture-thoughts">
    Record your voice, photograph handwriting, or type a few words.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect sources" icon="inbox" href="/en/collect-sources">
    Save links, images, books, and papers from the share sheet or Inbox.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sync and backup" icon="cloud" href="/en/sync-and-backup">
    Use personal iCloud sync and keep a separate ZIP backup.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Start small" icon="sprout" href="/en/think-at-your-pace">
    Try a small beginning without making your page perfect.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choose your rhythm" icon="calendar-clock" href="/en/routine">
    Set optional reminders for the days and times that suit you.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
Basic handwriting recognition and voice transcription run on your device. Optional advanced recognition and detailed monthly reflections use external services. Recognition does not overwrite your original. See [Data use](/en/privacy-and-data) for the differences.
</Note>
