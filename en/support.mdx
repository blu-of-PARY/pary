title: "Contact Galpi"
description: "How to get help with Galpi."
---

If you need help with Galpi or want to share feedback, email [help@pary.kr](mailto:help@pary.kr).

## Include these details

- App version and device model
- App and capture-language settings
- The screen and steps that led to the issue
- Any error message you saw

You do not need to send original recordings, handwriting photos, or personal material. Share a redacted example or describe the screen instead.

You can also check [Troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting) and [Data use](/en/privacy-and-data) first.
